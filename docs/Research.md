# Research: Men’s Garment Selection by Occasion

This research supports the colour/garment suggestion logic in the Style Advisor app. It summarizes men’s dress etiquette for the occasions currently supported.

## Wedding (close family vs guest)

**Close family (formal/black-tie leaning)**
- Dark suits: black, midnight blue, charcoal. Black/dark tuxedo appropriate for formal dress codes.
- Tie required; bow tie for black tie. Muted, classic colours; avoid loud patterns.
- Shirt: white or black (tux shirt), crisp collared dress shirt.
- Shoes: black patent/leather oxfords or loafers, polished. Belt (if worn) matches shoes. Watch conservative (metal matching other accessories).

**Guest (cocktail/semi-formal)**
- Suits: navy, charcoal, grey, seasonal colours. Avoid black for guests unless evening formal.
- Shirt: white or light blue.
- Tie: optional later in the evening; if worn, muted or patterned subtly.
- Patterns: solids, pinstripes subtle, avoid bold.
- Shoes: dress shoes/loafers, polished; belt matches shoes. Watch simple.

## Funeral

- Dark, somber colours only: black, charcoal, dark navy acceptable; dark brown okay in some settings.
- Suits: black or charcoal ideal; navy widely accepted. Avoid bright colours and loud patterns. Micro patterns/textures acceptable if subtle.
- Shirt: plain white or light blue, pressed.
- Tie: solid black, charcoal, dark navy, deep burgundy; subtle patterns only.
- Shoes: black or dark brown dress shoes, polished; belt matches shoes.
- Accessories: minimal watch, belt, tie. Avoid flashy items.

## Formal Business Meeting

- Suits: navy, charcoal, medium gray (wool/wool-blend). Black reserved for funerals/formal events.
- Shirt: white or light blue, crisp, collar structured.
- Tie: silk, solid or small pattern (repp stripes, polka dots), conservative colours (burgundy, navy, forest green). Avoid novelty prints.
- Match leather: belt colour = shoe colour. Shoes: black cap-toe Oxfords for conservative; dark brown with navy/gray. Socks match trousers.
- Watch: conservative dress watch (leather strap or metal), not flashy.

## Date / Smart Casual (Informal)

- Jacket optional. Trousers: khakis/chinos or suit pants; relaxed but neat.
- Shirt: long-sleeved button-down in solid light colours; polo acceptable in very casual/hot settings.
- Tie optional; solid conservative colours if worn.
- Shoes: dress shoes or clean loafers; no sneakers/athletic shoes.
- Belt matches shoes; minimal watch.

## Cross-cutting Principles

- Belt colour should match shoes.
- Watch strap colour should complement/transition into shoes or belt; metal case (silver/gold) should harmonize with other metal accessories (buckle, cufflinks).
- Formality reduces pattern intensity: plain/solid preferred for funeral and formal; checks/stripes more for business/informal.
- Black symbolizes sadness/formality: especially suitable for funeral and (close family) wedding; less preferred for business day-to-day unless conservative.
- Bright/high-contrast colours are generally inappropriate for funeral and formal weddings; acceptable accents in cocktail weddings/informal meetings.

## Women (high-level)

### Wedding (guest)
- Avoid white/ivory/champagne that could compete with the bride; prefer navy, darker jewel tones for evening, soft pastels florals for day.
- Dress length and formality should match dress code (black-tie gown, cocktail dress, semi-formal).
- Closed-toe heels or elegant flats; clutch; minimal jewelry.
- Hat/fascinator for daytime weddings optional.

### Funeral
- Dark, modest attire: black, charcoal, navy. Avoid bright colours and flashy accessories.
- Dress or suit with sleeves, modest neckline; closed-toe shoes.
- Keep patterns subtle; minimal jewelry.

### Formal Business Meeting
- Skirted or trouser suit in black/navy/charcoal; pressed blouse (white/blue).
- Closed-toe pumps; minimal jewelry; conservative watch.
- Avoid loud patterns, short skirts, flashy accessories.

### Date / Smart Casual (Informal)
- Tailored pants/skirt with blouse or knit; cardigan or blazer optional.
- Closed-toe flats/pumps; modest accessories.
- Neat, polished, classic rather than trendy.

## Couples / Coordinated Outfits

- Coordination is optional and should be subtle: a man’s tie or pocket square may echo (not necessarily identically match) his partner’s dress colour or an accent. Fully matching outfits are stylistic, not mandatory.
- At weddings, partners should complement the dress code and venue; avoid both wearing white or outshining the wedding party.
- Formal business: each partner dresses independently to their own professional standard (no need to match); ensure both are neat and conservative.
- Funeral: both partners follow somber, respectful dress independently.
- Accessories: harmonize metal tones and leather colours within the couple if desired, but priority is event appropriateness.


## Pocket Square (Men, optional)

- Placement: breast pocket; should not dominate the outfit.
- Colour rule: echo one colour from tie/shirt or suit, but avoid an identical match; white linen is the safest formal baseline. Silk in richer tones for formal wear; linen/cotton in lighter tones for day/casual.
- Pattern rule: plain for very formal/black-tie; subtle dots/stripes for business; bolder folds/patterns for informal. Keep pattern quieter than the tie unless deliberately expressive (informal).
- Coordination rule: if tie is patterned, pocket square should be solid or lightly textured (and vice versa); matching fold and colour accents.
- Accessories: fold style affects formality (flat fold formal; puff fold relaxed). Metal accents should harmonize with watch case.


## Jewelry (Women, optional)
- Footwear/accessory context; keep minimal for formal/business; statement pieces only for date/casual weddings.
- Metals should coordinate with watch case and handbag hardware.
- Avoid distracting noise at funerals; small earrings/necklace only.


## Garment-specific rules

### Shirt (men)
- Formal/business: white or light blue; avoid bold patterns.
- Wedding guest: white or light blue, muted.
- Funeral: white only (or very light blue), plain.
- Date/smart casual: relaxed colours, subtle patterns.

### Blouse (women)
- Formal/business: white, light blue, soft neutrals; minimal patterns.
- Wedding: silk/satin, pastel or jewel depending on time.
- Funeral: dark, modest.
- Date: expressive textures/colours; floral acceptable.

### Jacket / Blazer
- Men: navy/charcoal for business; black for funeral/formal; optional in informal/date.
- Women: tailored blazer elevates separates; neutral base; black/dark for funeral/formal.
- Formality reduces pattern; solids preferred.

### Tie (men)
- Formal/business: silk, conservative patterns (repp stripes, polka dots), colours complement suit/shirt.
- Wedding guest: muted; avoid overly bright.
- Funeral: solid black/charcoal/dark navy/deep burgundy, subtle.
- Date/smart casual: optional; bolder allowed.

### Pocket Square (men, optional)
- Echo one colour from tie/shirt; avoid identical exact match.
- White linen safest formal; silk richer formal; cotton/linen casual.
- Pattern quieter than tie (and vice versa).

### Pants / Trousers
- Men: suit-coloured dress pants for formal; chinos for informal/date.
- Women: neutral tailored; wide/straight/tapered; fabric by occasion.
- Black for funeral/formal; navy/charcoal/grey for business; lighter for date.

### Skirt (women)
- Length knee or longer for business/funeral; cocktail length for weddings/date.
- Pencil/A-line/pleated; neutral/solid for formal; prints for weddings/date.

### Dress (women)
- Full look; excludes pants/skirt/blouse.
- Fabric and colour by occasion; avoid white/ivory at weddings.
- Closed-toe shoes for funeral/formal.

### Shoes
- Men: black/brown leather matched to belt; oxfords formal; loafers casual.
- Women: closed-toe for business/funeral; heels/flats/wedges/boots by occasion; coordinate with bag/hardware.
- Metal/leather harmony.

### Belt (men)
- Match shoes colour and finish; slim buckle.

### Watch
- Strap colour match shoes/belt when possible; case metal (silver/gold) harmonize with other metals.
- Conservative for formal/business/funeral; expressive for date.

### Handbag / Clutch (women)
- Formal: small clutch, neutral or metallic.
- Business: structured tote, neutral.
- Date: can be statement but coordinated.

### Jewelry (women)
- Minimal for funeral/formal/business; statement allowed for date/wedding guest within taste.
- Metal tone coordinate with watch/bag hardware.
